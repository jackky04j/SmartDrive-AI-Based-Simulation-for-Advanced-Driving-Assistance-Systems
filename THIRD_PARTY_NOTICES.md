# Third-party notices

The Phase 2 driver-monitor integration uses the EAR, MAR, and head-pose helper
approach from the [Driver Drowsiness Detection project](https://github.com/neelanjan00/Driver-Drowsiness-Detection).
The reusable helper modules are kept under `src/phase2_carla/drowsiness/`; the
integration and simulation code in this repository are separate.

CARLA, Ultralytics YOLO, dlib, OpenCV, PyTorch, and their respective models are
subject to their own licences and terms. Review them before redistribution or
deployment.
